BURKESHOT v9 — SOURCE / LICENSING NOTES

The graphics source in src/ was written for this Burkeshot update.
range-hd.bundle.js includes Three.js 0.170.0 (MIT), its Sky addon and the original
Burkeshot scene. Three.js attribution/license is in assets/THREE-LICENSE.txt;
bundler-generated notices are in range-hd.bundle.js.LEGAL.txt.

The three PNGs in assets/ are original generated turf and tree imagery, not
GSPro assets. Their origins and prompts are recorded in assets/ASSET_NOTES.md.
Geometry, placement, scalar sand grain, grass blades and shading are generated
in code. The renderer does not download remote assets or execute CDN scripts.

GSPro (https://gsprogolf.com/) is a product-quality inspiration only.
No GSPro source, branding, course files, Unity assets or artwork are included.
This update does not claim identical fidelity, physics or compatibility.

SmartTrack Golf and the user-supplied App Store screenshots informed the V9
phone-first navigation, overlay-metric and session-list direction only. No
SmartTrack Golf logo, artwork, screenshots, source code or private assets are
included. Burkeshot keeps its own name, controls and visual identity.
